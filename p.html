What counts as a bid? One credit is used when you finalize a new bid. Editing, re-finalizing, or re-downloading a bid you already finalized is free, and drafts and previews never use credits.

How do free credits work? Every verified account gets 3 starter credits that never expire. From your second month, you also get 1 free monthly bid on your signup anniversary each month. Monthly credits don't roll over, and they're always used before starter credits.
