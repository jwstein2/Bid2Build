## How the allocator works [PARAM]

1. Rank paid-eligible buy units by opportunity score (report 01). Facebook and Instagram are bought together as Meta placements.
2. Each funded unit first gets its minimum viable test: the larger of the platform minimum and 50 clicks a month at base CPC [F01] [PARAM]. LinkedIn's floor is $10/day [CE025]; TikTok's ad-group floor is $20/day [CE029].
3. Channel limit by budget: $300 → 1, $500 → 1, $1,000 → 2, $2,500 → 3, $5,000 → 4 [PARAM]. Small budgets are concentrated, not spread.
4. Remaining money is split in proportion to score, up to test-stage caps (SEARCH $1,500, META $2,500, YOUTUBE $1,500, PARTNERS $1,000, LINKEDIN $1,000, TIKTOK $1,000) [PARAM]. An exploratory unit gets its minimum only.
5. From $1,000 a 5% reserve is held for broken ads and creative fixes [PARAM]. Money that hits every cap is held, not spent.

## Base-case economics in one line

Base LTV is $298 of gross profit per paying account [M] [F07] [F08] [F10]. Base CAC is $3,261 for search and $1,467 for Meta [M]. **At base assumptions no paid channel pays back.** These budgets buy learning. Only in the optimistic column do Meta, YouTube and partner CACs fall under the 10-month payback limit, and search stays above it (report 05). That is why spend stays small until experiments replace the assumptions [X10].

## Reading the scenarios

- **$300 and $500:** one channel, Google Search. These budgets can measure CTR, CPC and landing conversion, but cannot measure paid conversion inside 90 days [M]. If the Keyword Planner forecast shows higher CPC than the conservative assumption, rerun with Meta first [X01].
- **$1,000:** search plus a small Meta creative test, matching the creative concepts already briefed in ADS.md [P1:S14].
- **$2,500:** adds one exploratory creator deal; still no YouTube ads [M].
- **$5,000:** all four paid-eligible units. Meta at this level is the only line that approaches a readable paid-conversion sample in 90 days [M].
- **Compared with the existing $600 draft in ADS.md (Meta $400, YouTube $180):** this model puts search first because of intent and puts YouTube spend last, below Meta, because its CPC and measurement are least certain [G204]. The ADS.md stop and scale rules are kept unchanged [P1:S14].
