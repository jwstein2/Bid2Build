# 04 Organic Distribution Strategy

Built2Bid Growth OS · Phase 2 · model v2.0.0 (2026-11-23) · generated by scripts/render_reports.py, do not edit by hand.

Organic time is planned separately from paid spend. A channel with $0 paid budget can still get organic time [PARAM].
Organic score = 25% audience quality + 40% organic opportunity + 15% content cost + 20% measurability [PARAM]. Time is shared in proportion to the squared score so it concentrates on the strongest channels; repurpose-only channels are capped at 5% each [PARAM].

Hours are the owner's (or a helper's) weekly time: lean 5, base 10, committed 20 hours [PARAM].

## One asset, many channels

1. Record one real estimate walkthrough for a priority trade (screen recording with example data, as ADS.md requires) [P1:S14].
2. Publish the long version on YouTube with a UTM-tagged link to the matching /bids/[trade] page.
3. Cut 2 Shorts; repost them as Instagram and Facebook Reels and, at no extra effort, TikTok.
4. Turn the script into a written guide on the trade landing page for search.
5. Use the same explanation to answer questions in contractor communities, linking only where group rules allow.

## Organic rules

- Content follows the ADS.md copy rules: no testimonials or results that have not happened; no speed, profit, "win more jobs" or percentage claims [P1:E191].
- Every link carries utm_source and utm_medium=organic so /admin/marketing can attribute signups.
- Community posts disclose that the author works on Built2Bid.
- Partner and creator outreach is drafted for the owner; nothing is sent without approval (standing rule).
- Lifecycle email stays off until the owner approves it [X04]; CAN-SPAM applies to any marketing email [CE040].
