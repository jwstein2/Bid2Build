# 06 30/60/90-Day Experiment Plan

Built2Bid Growth OS · Phase 2 · model v2.0.0 (2026-11-23) · generated by scripts/render_reports.py, do not edit by hand.

Free experiments come first. No paid experiment starts until the measurement gate passes for that channel and the owner signs the ADS.md approval table.

## Days 1-30

### [X00] Measurement readiness gate (All paid)

- **Hypothesis:** Every paid click can be tied to signup, 7-day activation and paid subscription by channel and creative.
- **Actions:** Owner: GA4 key events (sign_up, pdf_exported) and Google Ads link; Meta privacy text, SQL, dataset, CAPI token and a test event seen in Events Manager; UTM QA in a private window per ADS.md.
- **Metric:** Test signup appears in GA4 DebugView, Google Ads conversions and Meta Test Events, and in /admin/marketing with the right utm_content.
- **Replaces:** G208
- **Sample:** 3 test signups per channel
- **Decision rule:** No paid test starts until this passes for that channel. [PARAM]
- **Cost:** $0; owner action needed: yes [PARAM]
### [X01] Search demand forecast (Google Search)

- **Hypothesis:** Enough monthly searches exist for tight contractor-estimating keywords to support the SEARCH cap.
- **Actions:** Owner opens Keyword Planner (free in a Google Ads account, no campaign) for 30-50 seed terms per priority trade; export volumes and top-of-page bid ranges.
- **Metric:** Monthly searches and bid ranges per keyword group.
- **Replaces:** F01 SEARCH, SEARCH cap, G202
- **Sample:** All seed terms
- **Decision rule:** If forecast CPC exceeds the conservative F01 value, the $300 and $500 scenarios switch to META (rerun the model). [PARAM]
- **Status:** Deferred 2026-11-23 by owner decision. The Google Ads account is held in the guided campaign setup, which cannot be left without entering billing; Expert Mode was not offered. Benchmark CPC (F01) stays until the owner decides to fund a search test. [F01]
- **Cost:** $0; owner action needed: yes [PARAM]
### [X02] Reach estimates (Meta, YouTube, LinkedIn)

- **Hypothesis:** Contractor-signal audiences are large enough for the planned spend.
- **Actions:** Owner reads the estimated audience size in Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads draft (unpublished) audiences.
- **Metric:** Estimated reach for each draft audience.
- **Replaces:** G202
- **Sample:** n/a
- **Decision rule:** Record in channels.json reachable_ad_audience. [PARAM]
- **Cost:** $0; owner action needed: yes [PARAM]
### [X03] Organic landing-page baseline (Organic search, direct)

- **Hypothesis:** Trade landing pages convert organic visitors at a measurable rate before paid traffic arrives.
- **Actions:** Read GA4 landing_view to sign_up by trade page for 30 days.
- **Metric:** Visit-to-signup rate by page.
- **Replaces:** F03 anchor
- **Sample:** At least 300 landing views in total
- **Decision rule:** If below 2%, fix the landing page before any paid test (paid traffic converts worse than organic). [PARAM]
- **Cost:** $0; owner action needed: no [PARAM]
### [X04] Lifecycle emails on (Email)

- **Hypothesis:** Lifecycle emails A-E raise 7-day activation.
- **Actions:** Owner approves LIFECYCLE_EMAIL_MODE; compare matured cohorts before and after.
- **Metric:** 7-day activation rate of matured cohorts.
- **Replaces:** F04
- **Sample:** All new signups; directional until about 100 per arm
- **Decision rule:** Keep on unless unsubscribes or complaints spike. [PARAM]
- **Cost:** $0; owner action needed: yes [PARAM]
## Days 15-90

### [X05] First paid search test (Google Search)

- **Hypothesis:** Contractor-intent keywords produce signups that activate at or above the base assumption.
- **Actions:** One campaign, exact/phrase match on estimate-template and estimating-software terms for the priority trades, strong negatives for homeowner terms; landing on matching /bids/[trade] page. Starts only after X00 passes and the owner approves the ADS.md gate table.
- **Metric:** CTR, CPC, landing conversion, cost per 7-day activated contractor, paid accounts.
- **Replaces:** F01 SEARCH, F02 SEARCH, F03 SEARCH, F05 SEARCH
- **Sample:** At least 150 clicks before judging landing conversion
- **Decision rule:** ADS.md stop rules; continue past day 90 only if cost per activated contractor is at or below the base-model value. [PARAM]
- **Cost:** scenario budget; owner action needed: yes [PARAM]
## Days 31-60

### [X07] Activation onboarding test (Product)

- **Hypothesis:** Opening the builder on the trade picked from the landing page raises 7-day activation.
- **Actions:** Compare activation by entry path (trade chip vs generic) in existing reports; no app change in this phase.
- **Metric:** 7-day activation by entry path.
- **Replaces:** F04
- **Sample:** Directional
- **Decision rule:** Feed findings to Phase 8 (conversion). [PARAM]
- **Cost:** $0; owner action needed: no [PARAM]
## Days 31-90

### [X06] Meta creative test (Meta)

- **Hypothesis:** At least one of concepts B, A or E delivers activated contractors at a lower cost than search.
- **Actions:** One campaign, one ad set, three creatives from ADS.md; optimize on CompleteRegistration only after the CAPI test event is verified.
- **Metric:** Cost per 7-day activated contractor by utm_content.
- **Replaces:** F01 META, F03 META, F05 META
- **Sample:** About 2,000 impressions per creative before the CTR stop rule applies
- **Decision rule:** ADS.md stop rules per ad and ad set. [PARAM]
- **Cost:** scenario budget ($1,000 and up); owner action needed: yes [PARAM]
### [X08] Creator partner pilot (Partnerships)

- **Hypothesis:** A trade creator delivers activated contractors at or below search cost.
- **Actions:** Shortlist creators; owner approves outreach and any fee; creator link with utm_medium=creator; FTC disclosure required.
- **Metric:** Clicks, signups, activated and paid by utm_campaign (creator handle).
- **Replaces:** F01 PARTNERS, F03 PARTNERS, G206
- **Sample:** One deal
- **Decision rule:** Repeat only if cost per activated contractor is at or below search. [PARAM]
- **Cost:** $500-1,000 per deal (assumption; only in the $2,500+ scenarios); owner action needed: yes [PARAM]
## Days 61-90

### [X10] Paid conversion read and scale/stop (All paid)

- **Hypothesis:** Observed funnel is within the model range.
- **Actions:** Pull channel and creative tables from /admin/marketing; compute cost per activated and per paid; rerun build.py with observed values.
- **Metric:** Activated-to-paid rate, CAC, payback by channel.
- **Replaces:** F05, F06, F08
- **Sample:** Paid conversion is only readable after about 30 activated contractors per channel; below that, report as directional
- **Decision rule:** Scale only per the ADS.md scale rule; otherwise hold or cut to $0 paid. [PARAM]
- **Cost:** $0; owner action needed: yes [PARAM]
## Days 1-90

### [X09] Organic video cadence (YouTube (organic))

- **Hypothesis:** Weekly how-to estimating videos produce search traffic and signups within 90 days.
- **Actions:** Publish per the organic plan; track YouTube Studio search traffic and utm-tagged description links.
- **Metric:** Views from YouTube search, link clicks, signups by utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=organic.
- **Replaces:** organic opportunity scores
- **Sample:** 12 videos
- **Decision rule:** Shift organic hours toward the format with the most signups per hour. [PARAM]
- **Cost:** $0; owner action needed: no [PARAM]
## Day 90+

### [X11] Retention first read (All)

- **Hypothesis:** Month-1 and month-2 retention are at or above the base churn assumption.
- **Actions:** Count renewals (subscription_renewed) and cancellations by cohort.
- **Metric:** Monthly churn by cohort.
- **Replaces:** F07
- **Sample:** Needs 6+ months for a real estimate
- **Decision rule:** Update LTV; no scaling decision on churn under 3 months of data. [PARAM]
- **Cost:** $0; owner action needed: no [PARAM]
