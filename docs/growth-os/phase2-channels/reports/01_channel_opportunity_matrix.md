# 01 Channel Opportunity Matrix

Built2Bid Growth OS · Phase 2 · model v2.0.0 (2026-11-23) · generated by scripts/render_reports.py, do not edit by hand.

Each channel is scored 1 to 5 on seven weighted dimensions [PARAM]. Weights: Audience quality 20%, Purchase intent 20%, Expected CAC 20%, Content cost 10%, Measurability 15%, Competitive pressure 5%, Evidence confidence 10% [PARAM].
Expected CAC is computed for paid channels from the funnel assumptions [F01] [F03] [F04] [F05] [F06] against base LTV [M]; for owned and organic channels it is judgment.
Adoption is never used as a weight. A platform with the most users can still score low if its users are not contractor owners with an estimating need [INF].

## Paid-spend gates [PARAM]

- A channel can receive paid budget only if measurability is at least 3 and the weighted score is at least 2.25 [PARAM].
- Below $2,500/month, paid tests go only to channels with evidence confidence of at least 3. From $2,500 one exploratory channel (evidence 2) may get its minimum viable test, and no more [PARAM].
- A channel that fails the gates gets $0 paid spend and can still get organic time (report 04) [PARAM].
