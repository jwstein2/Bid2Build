Support time is the largest direct cost in the model. It is priced at $40/hour even though James does
the work today, because founder time is not free and will have to be paid for at scale.

The "without Financials revenue" column keeps the Financials usage costs in, so it is slightly
pessimistic.

## Not included anywhere below

Advertising and lead acquisition, founder compensation, the partner's 20% of net (definition unknown,
see `PARTNER-CHECKLIST.md`), accounting/legal reviews (FCC Phase 0 estimates about $312/month
amortized), insurance (general liability + E&O/cyber, typically $100 to $250/month for a small SaaS;
get quotes), income taxes, and Stripe Tax registration fees in other states. Contribution margin is
not net profit.

## Break-even

The fixed platform at 100 companies is about $154/month. That is covered by about 11 Standard
monthly subscribers (about $14.69 contribution each), before any overhead in the list above.
