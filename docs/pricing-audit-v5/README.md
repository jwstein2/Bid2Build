### Verified correct (no change needed)

- **Live Stripe prices match the code exactly** (Verified 2026-11-17): Standard `price_1Ub7SE…NtPyHfuD` $19.99/mo and `…gxszXflS` $149/yr; PM add-on $4.99/mo and $49.99/yr; PM editor $1.99/mo; Enterprise base `price_1UcfEA…WB9rBWpa` $49.99/mo; Enterprise seat `price_1UcfEA…sxQTkAIb` **graduated**, tiers up_to 2 @ $19.99, up_to 7 @ $17.99, inf @ $15.99. Every checkout re-verifies amounts against code (`priceMismatch`, `enterpriseSeatMismatch`, `pmPriceMismatch`).
- **Graduated tiers are applied per seat position, not "all seats at the last rate".** Code and an independent Stripe-algorithm re-implementation agree for every headcount from 1 to 250 (`tests/billing-correctness-v5.test.ts`).
- **Seats are never billed on invitation.** Invites can only use seats already paid for; pending invites reserve a seat (`company_seats_used`), and activation is refused above the licensed count in the database (`no_seats_available`).
- **Removing seats or PM editors:** refused below seats in use or editors assigned; no credit for the paid period (PR #89, `proration_behavior: "none"`). The lower count takes effect in Stripe immediately and the next invoice is at the new count. Since only unused seats can be removed, nobody loses access.
- **Adding seats or editors:** previewed with Stripe's exact proration, then invoiced immediately with `always_invoice` + `error_if_incomplete`, idempotency keys, and a "seats changed" guard against two admins racing.
- **PM editors require an active licensed member** (`pm_entitlement` joins `company_members` with `status = 'active'`); the earliest assignments win if the limit drops. Changing a member's role or permissions does not touch Stripe.
- **Webhook:** signature-verified, idempotent per event id, re-fetches the subscription, ignores out-of-order events, and has 0 unprocessed or errored events in production.
- **Free tier is enforced server-side** in one transaction (`finalize_bid`, row lock on `credit_accounts`, unique ledger indexes): 3 starter credits once per verified email (hashed, survives account deletion), 1 monthly credit per signup-anniversary cycle, no rollover, monthly spent before starter, re-downloads and edits of an already-counted bid are free.
- **Complimentary access is excluded from paid revenue** and cannot be extended into paid seats by the customer (seat/editor routes refuse comp companies).

## 4. Financial seat entitlement design (deliverable 5)

Proposed only; Phase 1 is being built in the Financial Command Center thread, which owns the
architecture. Requirements to carry into that work:

- Financial access is an **assignment on an existing licensed member** (Enterprise) or on the
  account holder (Standard). It never creates a platform seat, and it is independent of PM editor
  assignment.
- Billing: base item (qty 1, includes 3 assignments) + flat per-unit seat item (qty = assigned − 3) on
  the **same** subscription (one invoice, see §5). `sync.ts` must exclude the Financials price ids
  when picking the plan item, as it does for PM (the highest regression risk).
- Reassignment by `manage_billing` holders only; changing who holds an assignment is free; buying a
  4th+ assignment previews then invoices like PM editors; removal frees the slot with no credit.
- Cancellation: records become read-only, exportable, never deleted (accounting retention 3 to 7
  years).

## 5. Consolidated billing (deliverable 7)

Already the architecture: Standard + PM, and Enterprise + seats + editors, are items on **one**
subscription, so the customer gets one invoice and pays one 30¢ card fee. Financials should join the
same subscription. A separate subscription would add 30¢ per month (1.2% of $24.99) and a second
charge on the customer's card statement. Contractor customer payments must never go through this
Stripe account (Stripe Connect, approval required, per the FCC rules).

## 6. Annual pricing (deliverable 8)

From ECONOMICS.md: monthly nets $18.87/month after fees; annual nets $142.59/year ($11.88/month,
63% of monthly). Annual wins only if annual customers renew well: at 5% monthly churn on the monthly
plan, annual customers must renew at 62% or better to be worth as much; at 3% churn, 77%.

**Recommendation (Decision D2):** keep $149/year for everyone who buys it, with the price honored at
renewal for as long as they stay (grandfathered). Relabel it as a **launch price** for new customers
and review it after 6 months of real renewal and churn data. A likely long-term price is $179 to
$199/year (25% to 17% off), which is closer to SaaS norms. Do not copy the 38% discount to Financials:
FCC Phase 0 suggested $249/year (17% off), decide after real costs exist.

## 14. Scalability (deliverable 22)

**No load test was run.** Production load testing needs approval, and representative staging data
does not exist yet. Built2Bid **cannot** claim support for 2,500 or 10,000 companies without one.

What is known: request handlers are stateless Netlify functions; the database is the bottleneck.
Micro compute allows 60 Postgres connections and 24 are in use at rest. Expected first limits, in
order: Micro compute CPU and connections (upgrade to Small, then Medium, around 500 to 1,000 active
companies), PDF rendering time in functions, then storage egress for photos.

Proposed plan (**Decision D5**): create a Supabase branch (or a second small project) seeded with
synthetic companies (70% solo, 20% 3-user, 8% 10-user, 2% 20-user), run k6 against a Netlify
preview at 100, 500 and 1,000 company equivalents, record p95 latency, error rate, DB CPU and
connection count, then decide compute size. Estimated cost: one day of a Small branch plus test time.

## 15. Security and compliance (deliverable 23)

Verified with the Supabase security advisor (2026-11-17): no RLS-disabled tables. Warnings: 14
SECURITY DEFINER trigger functions are exposed as RPCs to `anon` (calling a trigger function directly
fails, so this is noise, but revoking EXECUTE is tidy); 4 functions without a fixed `search_path`;
130 definer functions callable by signed-in users (by design: they check `auth.uid()`); 61 tables
with RLS on and no policies (service-role only, intended); leaked-password protection off. No card
data touches Built2Bid (Stripe Checkout/Portal).

Recommended before Financials holds real accounting data: PITR + off-site storage backup, a written
incident response and restore drill, a CPA review of the financial workflows, attorney review of the
Terms and e-sign flow, and cyber/E&O insurance quotes. Running without an office does not remove
these obligations.
