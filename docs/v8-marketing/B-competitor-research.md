## Reference companies (patterns, not competitors)

|---|---|---|

| Acquisition.com | Free courses, no gate, next step beside the content | Academy and Learn should end every lesson with a pre-filled bid (Batch 3) |
| Webflow | "Get started" + "Talk to sales", case-study metrics | Team path later; metrics only once real |

## What we deliberately did not copy

- User counts, review counts and revenue-growth claims (we have no verified numbers).
- Demo-first gating (our advantage is building a real bid with no account).
- Platform-wide headlines ("run your whole business") that hide the first job.
