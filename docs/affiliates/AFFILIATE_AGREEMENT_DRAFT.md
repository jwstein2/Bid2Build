Built2Bid L.L.C. ("Built2Bid", "we") and you ("Affiliate", "you").

## 1. Eligibility and approval

You may join only after Built2Bid approves your application or invitation. We may approve or decline any applicant for any lawful reason, including audience relevance, content quality and professionalism. Subscriber or follower counts alone do not decide approval. You must be at least 18 and able to enter contracts. For now, commission payments are available only to approved affiliates who are U.S. persons with a U.S. bank account.

## 2. Independent relationship

You are an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint venture, franchise or agency relationship. You have no authority to make promises, sign agreements or accept payments for Built2Bid. You control your own content, schedule and methods, and you are responsible for your own expenses, taxes and insurance.

## 3. Qualified referral

A "Qualified Referral" is a new Built2Bid customer account that (a) is created through your unique referral link while the visit is within the attribution window shown in your dashboard (currently 90 days), (b) is not you, your business, a household member, or an account you control, (c) did not already have a Built2Bid account or paid subscription, and (d) is not attributed to a Built2Bid sales representative or another affiliate first.

## 4. Attribution ownership

Each customer has one referral owner: the verified referral source recorded when the customer created the account. Later clicks, coupons or links do not change ownership. Built2Bid may review and correct attribution for fraud, error or a documented dispute, with a written record. Decisions on disputed attribution are made by an authorized Built2Bid administrator and you may ask for a review.

## 5. Commission calculation

For each Qualified Referral, you may earn one Annual Commission for each Eligible Year. An Annual Commission equals one month of the customer's eligible paid subscription value at that time, calculated from the amount actually paid before tax, after discounts and refunds, for recurring subscription items (including additional paid seats). For annual plans, the monthly value is the annual eligible amount divided by 12 unless the published program policy states otherwise. Taxes, failed or unpaid invoices, refunded or charged-back amounts, one-time fees and other excluded items are never commissionable. Amounts are rounded to the nearest cent.

## 6. Ten-year maximum term

Year 1 begins on the date of the customer's first qualifying paid subscription payment. Each later Eligible Year falls on that date's anniversary. There are at most ten (10) Annual Commissions per Qualified Referral. The ten-year period does not restart because of upgrades, downgrades, billing-cycle changes, pauses, cancellation or reactivation.

## 7. Annual payout frequency

Commissions are earned and paid annually per customer, not monthly. No commission is paid in advance for future years.

## 8. Thirty-day holding period

Each Annual Commission is held for thirty (30) days after the qualifying payment, then re-checked for refunds, chargebacks, customer eligibility and your compliance with this Agreement before it becomes payable.

## 9. Cancellation, refunds and chargebacks

An Eligible Year requires that the customer's subscription is active and paid for the anniversary date. If the customer cancels, stops paying or is refunded, that year's commission is not earned, and eligibility for later years ends unless the customer is paying again on a later anniversary within the original ten-year period. A refund or chargeback during the holding period cancels that commission. _**[COUNSEL]** decide whether Built2Bid may recover a commission already paid when the related payment is later refunded or charged back, and the notice required._ Until counsel approves, Built2Bid does not deduct or recover paid commissions.

## 10. Subscription pricing changes

Built2Bid sets its own prices and plans and may change them at any time. Each Annual Commission uses the customer's eligible value at that anniversary, which may be higher or lower than in earlier years.

## 11. Payout method and taxes

Payable commissions are paid by ACH through Built2Bid's payment provider to the bank account you connect on the provider's secure page. Built2Bid never asks for or stores your full bank account number. A minimum payable balance may apply; balances below it carry forward. You must provide a valid IRS Form W-9 before payment. Built2Bid will issue tax information returns (such as Form 1099) when required by law and may apply backup withholding only where the law requires it. You are responsible for your own taxes.

## 12. Fraud and prohibited conduct

You may not: refer yourself or accounts you control; use cookie stuffing, forced clicks, hidden or misleading links, or incentivized sign-ups not approved in writing; bid on Built2Bid trademarks in paid search; create fake accounts or reviews; or manipulate attribution. Commissions resulting from prohibited conduct are not earned.

## 13. Brand and trademarks

We grant you a limited, revocable, non-exclusive license to use the approved Built2Bid name, logos and materials from the Affiliate Resource Center only to promote Built2Bid under this Agreement. You may not imply you are Built2Bid or speak for it, register confusing domains or handles, or alter our marks.

## 14. FTC endorsement disclosures

You must clearly and conspicuously disclose your financial relationship with Built2Bid in each piece of content that promotes it, in the content itself (for example, said in the video and shown on screen), not only in a description or profile. You may only describe experiences you actually had and may not make claims Built2Bid cannot support.

## 15. YouTube and other platforms

You must follow the rules of each platform you use, including YouTube's paid promotion disclosure setting ("includes paid promotion") when applicable, and each platform's community guidelines and advertising policies.

## 16. Marketing and anti-spam

You must comply with CAN-SPAM, the TCPA, state privacy laws and similar laws. No unsolicited bulk email, texts or calls promoting Built2Bid. You may not buy or scrape contact lists to promote Built2Bid.

## 17. Confidentiality and data protection

Non-public information you receive (including dashboard data about referred customers) is confidential and may only be used to manage your participation. Built2Bid shows you privacy-safe customer identifiers only. You must not try to identify or contact referred customers about their Built2Bid account.

## 18. Termination

Either party may end this Agreement at any time with written notice. (a) Accrued commissions: Annual Commissions that became payable before termination are paid on the normal schedule. (b) Future conditional commissions: if Built2Bid terminates without cause or you resign, Annual Commissions for Qualified Referrals made before termination continue to be earned and paid on their normal schedule for the rest of the ten-year term while the customer remains eligible, unless this Agreement provides otherwise. _**[COUNSEL]** confirm or revise post-termination treatment._ (c) Material misconduct: if Built2Bid terminates for fraud, prohibited conduct, a material breach not cured within a reasonable notice period, or illegal activity, commissions that are not yet paid are held for review and are forfeited to the extent they relate to the misconduct, and no future commissions are earned. Built2Bid will give written reasons.

## 19. Disputes and corrections

You may raise a question about attribution, a commission or a payout from your dashboard within 180 days. Built2Bid will review it and respond in writing. Errors in either direction will be corrected.

## 20. Program changes and amendments

Built2Bid may change the program prospectively with at least 30 days' written notice. Changes do not reduce commissions that already became payable, and do not reduce the commission terms that apply to Qualified Referrals made before the change unless you agree in writing or the change is required by law. _**[COUNSEL]** confirm._ Continued participation after the notice period means you accept the change for new referrals.

## 21. Limitation of liability

Except for amounts owed under this Agreement, neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages. Built2Bid's total liability is limited to commissions earned and unpaid. Nothing guarantees any level of earnings.

## 22. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Minnesota, without regard to conflict-of-laws rules. _**[COUNSEL]** venue, arbitration, notice addresses._
